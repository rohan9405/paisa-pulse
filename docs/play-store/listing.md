## Full description (4,000-character limit)

Your money, one day at a time.

How much can you spend today? drip turns your budget into one daily number.
Set aside bills, savings and investments first. The remaining amount becomes a daily allowance that refreshes at midnight, India time. Spend less and the unused amount rolls forward. Spend more and future days account for the difference.

AUTOMATIC BANK SMS TRACKING
With your permission, drip reads supported Indian bank SMS alerts on your phone and turns them into transactions. It recognises alerts from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis and Kotak. Categories and merchant names help you make sense of your spending. OTPs, offers and unrelated messages are ignored by the parser. SMS access is optional: manual entries and CSV import work without it.

BUDGET YOUR WAY
Choose a planned daily budget, a budget based on recorded income and payments, or simply track spending without setting budget amounts. Set a tracking start date and account for bills and savings without losing your history when plans change.

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
Review your daily allowance, carried balance, spending categories, recurring payments and week or month summaries. Morning and evening briefings give you a quick view of your day. Review recipients and remember categories for future transactions.

YOUR BUDGET ON THE HOME SCREEN
Add the Android home-screen widget to check your daily totals. With SMS access enabled and a widget installed, a short background task can refresh supported bank transactions even while the app is closed. Android may delay background refreshes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up, advertising or analytics SDKs. Your bank SMS and financial records are processed and stored on your device, not uploaded to Soft Orbit Apps. The app can download a signed settings file; it does not upload your transaction database. An optional app lock uses Android's biometric or screen-lock system.

KEEP A COPY
Export your records, restore a backup, or choose a folder for automatic backups. Backups are not encrypted by drip; protect the folder you choose. Cloud-synced folders are handled by your chosen provider.

TRY THE DEMO
Explore drip with clearly labelled fictional transactions before setting up your own budget. Demo and personal data stay separate.

Built for Indian rupees and India time. Android 8 or newer. SMS formats vary between banks, so review imported transactions against your bank records. drip is a budgeting tool, not a bank, payment service or financial adviser.
